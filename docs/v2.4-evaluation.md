# v2.4 evaluation

v2.4 is the code review of 2026-11 (SEPAR_FIXES.md). It was measured on variant seed
**335384967**, recorded in docs/variant-seeds.md after the review's Part 1 fixes (which change no
evaluated decision) and before any Part 2 code. The variants were generated once (1035; sha256 of
the file `d95dfc0c110210bb…`) and replayed in `v2.3.1-base` (tag: v2.3 plus the Part 1 fixes) and in
`v2.4` (tag). **No weight changed.** Part 2 proposes three structural changes, design decisions
A80 to A82; each is behind a policy flag that keeps the old behaviour until the owner approves, so
`v2.4` with its default policy is the old behaviour. They were evaluated on the same variants with
their flag on, so the decision about them can be made on numbers. Data:
`docs/evaluation/v2.3.1-base-seed-335384967.jsonl`, `v2.4-seed-335384967.jsonl`,
`v2.4+A81-…`, `v2.4+A82-…`, `v2.4+A81+A82-…`, `comparison-seed-335384967.md` (the two tags) and
`comparison-seed-335384967-flags.md` (the flags). `make reproduce` regenerates the first comparison
(and checks it against the committed rows); the flag runs are
`scripts/dump_matrix.py --set math.decision_rule=expected_cost` (and `math.origin_combination=noisy_or`).

- **A81 (noisy-OR Origin)** changes no variant of this seed on its own; with A82 it turns one
  effective hold back into a block (S33 combo-11: two labels of 0.52 each; their noisy-OR, 0.77,
  raises the Origin contribution and L from −3.71 to −3.01, just above the expected-cost block
  boundary of −3.012).
- **A82 (expected cost)** moves 20 malicious variants from block to effective hold (all
  seed-decided; McNemar on blocked: 20 / 0, p = 1.9e-6): stories between −3.91 and −3.01 nats,
  where a hold costs less than a block under the policy's own costs. It moves 17 labelled benign
  variants from block to hold. It lets **one** malicious variant through: S27 timing=beyond_window,
  whose log-odds lie between the old hold threshold (−4.942) and the expected-cost boundary
  (−4.934); that is G4 coming back, and approving A82 means restoring the G4 entry. Contained:
  709 → 708 (McNemar 1 / 0, p = 1.0).
- **A80** acts on the browser path only (below).
- The golden catalog reaches its expected `decision` in **60 of 60** scenarios in every setting
  (default, A81, A82, both).

## Falco baseline (measured 2026-11-24)

`sudo scripts/falco_baseline.sh` ran the 28 runnable Attack Lab templates (22 attack stand-ins,
the known limit included, and 6 benign commands) in the lab sandbox, each in its own session, while
Falco 0.45.2 (modern eBPF, container plugin loaded, no service) watched. Result:
`docs/evaluation/falco-baseline-v2.4.json` (rule names, priorities, program, event, protocol and
remote port only; the raw alerts stay in the ignored `.falco-baseline/`).

| Pass | Active rules | Attacks alerted (of 22) | Benign alerted (of 6) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| default (stable) | 25 | 0 | 0 |
| stable + incubating + sandbox | 81 of 95 | 3 | 1 |
| Separ v2.4 (reference run) | | 21 held or blocked | 0 |

The three attack alerts: `revshell_bash` (System procs network activity, on the shell's UDP/53
name lookups; the `.invalid` host never resolves), `suid_copy` (Set Setuid or Setgid bit),
`wipe_history` (Delete or rename shell history). The benign alert: `pypi` (Unexpected UDP Traffic,
`curl` to UDP/443). Each pass first required an alert on a positive control (`cat /etc/shadow`).
Not run: 7 attack templates and the 18 controls (they would change the real system). The templates
were written by Separ's author; the comparison is narrow (paper, Evaluation).

## Not covered

- A80 to A82 are measured here, not approved: until the owner approves them the default policy
  keeps the old behaviour, and the CLAUDE.md freeze table lists them as proposals.
- The packages were built and their installer run on the development host (Python 3.14); the
  install in Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13 and Rocky 9 containers runs in CI, not here.
- `LinuxEnforcer` is tested on a fake process table and a fake `nft`; the opt-in
  `pytest -m root` test was not run (it needs root in a VM).
- The live-path changes (items 10, 13, 14) are not part of the variant generator, which mutates
  replayed events; they are measured by their tests and the lab controls.
- 335384967 is the active seed of `make reproduce` (tags `v2.3.1-base` and `v2.4`).
